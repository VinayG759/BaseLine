Baseline: a health record that notices

Photograph your family's lab reports. Baseline remembers every result and tells you, in your language, when a number keeps moving the wrong way.

Open Baseline →
  • Reports sit in a drawer.
  • Each one gets a glance.
  • The warning is in the change BETWEEN reports.

How It Works

1. Photograph

Snap a photo of your printed lab test report with your camera.

2. Remembers

Reads every number and connects it to your earlier reports.

3. Notices

Charts each result against its normal range and points out numbers that keep rising.
